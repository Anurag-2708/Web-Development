Fried Rice

Description

Fried rice is a flavorful and colorful dish made by stir-frying cooked rice with vegetables,
sauces, and sometimes eggs or meat. Originating from Asian cuisine, it's a quick way to turn
leftover rice into a delicious, wholesome meal. Each grain is coated with savory flavors from
soy sauce and seasonings, while veggies add crunch and freshness.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice (preferably cold)
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 small onion (chopped)
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables (carrot, beans, capsicum, peas)
  • 2 garlic cloves (minced)
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp vinegar (optional)
  • 1 tsp chili sauce (optional)
  • 2 eggs (optional)
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Spring onions for garnish

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a wok or large pan. Scramble the eggs (if using) and set aside.
  2. Add a little more oil, then sauté garlic and onions until fragrant.
  3. Add chopped vegetables and stir-fry on high heat for 2-3 minutes.
  4. Add cooked rice, soy sauce, vinegar, chili sauce, salt, and pepper. Stir well to mix evenly.
  5. Toss in the scrambled eggs and mix again.
  6. Garnish with chopped spring onions and serve hot.
